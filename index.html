04 / Manifesto

We build at the intersection of science and technology.

We believe the most important scientific discoveries of the future will emerge where disciplines meet. Biology needs better computational methods, artificial intelligence needs to understand the complex knowledge of the real world, and software is becoming part of the infrastructure of modern science.

So we don't just study problems — we design tools, build databases, ship software, and document the process along the way.

07 / About

We are building at the intersection of science and technology.

Filll Studio (飞荔科学) is an independent science and technology studio. We work at the intersection of artificial intelligence, life science, algorithms, and software engineering, and we try to connect these fields together.

We believe the most important scientific discoveries of the future will emerge where disciplines meet: biology needs more advanced computational methods, artificial intelligence needs to understand the complex knowledge of the real world, and software is becoming part of the infrastructure of modern science.

So we don't just study problems — we design tools, build databases, develop software, and document the journey.
